About the project

The work brings together the main stages of a 3D production: environment creation, object modelling, materials and textures, character rigging and animation, camera composition and preparation of the final result.

Components

  • Natural environment with trees, rocks, grass and sky
  • Materials, textures and UV mapping
  • Character rigging and running animation
  • Object animation and camera movement
  • Environmental and sound effects

Technologies

Blender 3D Modelling Rigging Animation Materials and UV Python Sound design

Challenges and learning

The project provided practice in building a coherent scene, configuring materials, controlling a rig, shaping animation timing and coordinating camera, environment and audio.
