A little about me

Learning through practice and people

I enjoy teaching myself new skills and putting them into practice, whether I am programming or learning an instrument. I also love working in a team, exchanging ideas and building something together. At college, I want to bring that curiosity and willingness to contribute.

My next chapter

Why the United States?

My interest in the United States comes from the room I see to grow: as a student, as a person and in my career. Portugal gave me a strong foundation. I want to build on it in a new environment, explore more possibilities in technology and discover paths I have not yet imagined.

Space to explore and grow

I hope to learn from people with different experiences, try new subjects and connect my studies with practical work. I see studying in the U.S. as a chance to become more independent, expand my professional network and work towards a career with an international outlook.

A place for my whole story

I find the strong emphasis on grades and examination results in Portugal’s national admissions process too narrow a measure of a student’s potential. I value academic achievement, but I believe initiative, practical experience, creativity and contributions to a team also deserve recognition. This is why I am drawn to U.S. colleges that take a holistic approach, considering a candidate’s achievements, circumstances and potential together.
