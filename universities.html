From practice to foundations

What I want to study

I want to study Computer Science or Computer Engineering, with room to explore software, embedded systems and robotics. After connecting a Flutter app to an ESP32-powered robotic hand in ARMIAC, my next step is to deepen my knowledge of algorithms, mathematics, electronics and control — understanding not just how to make something work, but how to test, explain and improve it.

Academic motivation

Building is the beginning, not the end

ARMIAC brought programming, electronics and physical movement together in one project. I want to bring that practical experience to questions that require deeper investigation: how to make a system more robust, evaluate its performance and design more accessible interactions. I am looking for an education that teaches me to ask better questions as well as find solutions.

A personal choice

Why I want to study and live in the U.S.

I want studying in the United States to be more than a change of country: an opportunity to become more independent, live and learn in English, and build relationships with people from different backgrounds. I want to participate in university life and get to know the country through everyday experience, contributing to the community where I study.

Contributing to campus

What I bring from Portugal

I bring a technical education, a working prototype and internship experience at XPC. Programming and robotics competitions and representing my school have given me opportunities to work in teams and present ideas. I want to bring that willingness to build, share what I learn and collaborate with classmates from other disciplines.

Next steps

What I want to do at college

Deepen my foundations in computing and engineering; seek faculty-mentored research; join robotics teams and student projects; and keep developing interests beyond technology, including music. I want to graduate with greater knowledge and a greater ability to contribute.

The learning environment I am looking for

Why the United States?

Portugal gave me the foundation to reach this point. I am not looking to the U.S. because I believe research, innovation or strong degree programs exist only there. I am looking for a combination of curriculum, research and campus life that fits my interests — one I need to evaluate at each institution.
